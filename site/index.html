VAH

Worldbuilding Archive · 2053 · officially in production

The World

2053. Humanity lives alongside VAH — bio-synthetic hero chassis, DNA-locked to the people wearing them. A single global authority, the Asosiasi Global, keeps order and licenses the agencies that manage the era's biggest industry: Hero Idols. Heroes who fight, and idols who perform. Sometimes both at once.

This archive introduces the world only. Story and chapter content stays in the workshop.

16 Sectors, 6 Active

The Asosiasi Global divides the world into 16 sectors. The story lives in six of them:

CodeSectorKnown for
area_01ASEAN SectorMain setting. Dense Southeast Asian megacities.
area_02East SectorAdministrative heart of the Asosiasi Global.
area_04Eurasian Industrial ZoneHeavy industry and chassis production.
area_06Han SectorTechnology hub.

The darkest of them all: Dark Metro (alias Neo-Batavia) — high-crime cyberpunk megacity, neon and acid rain, the busiest proving ground in Southeast Asia.

18 Agencies, 4 That Matter

The world recognizes 18 major corporations. Four carry the story:

CodeAgencyType
corp_01HelloVAHMega-agency. Global Hero Idol industry leader. Demands a perfect image from every talent.
corp_02Elemental SchoolElite agency. Bespoke chassis, spiritual-cyber aesthetics, atomic-precision nano weapons.
corp_03Lunar WorldOpen-source, officially licensed, deliberately left unsupervised as a social pressure valve. Run by a digital server gang styled after Akatsuki.
corp_04Demon SyndicateDark syndicate. Illegal chassis production, digital espionage, forbidden tech. The main antagonist force.

The other 14 — Sky Forge, Jade Ascension, Starlight Nine, Iron Willow, Liberty Nexus, Pacific Rim, Artisan Circle, Dune Sovereign, Oasis Order, Spice Route, Coral Vanguard, Verdant Pact, Frost Archive, Nomad Circuit — appear in the world's background.

Technology & Concepts

  • Sasis — bio-synthetic hero chassis, DNA-locked to a single wearer.
  • Link-In — full login into digital servers; inside, everyone walks as an avatar.
  • Induced-REM Pod — sleep-state tech for extended server dives.
  • NeuReader — neural interface hardware of the era.
  • Negatate Being — the infection class of threat. Devil-Grade and above.

The Production

VAH is built as a webtoon/manga in 9:16 vertical format, planned across roughly 10 chapters, produced inside an open AI-assisted workshop where every workflow is visible and structured.

  • 193 registered character entries (canon + placeholders) in a structured character database
  • Structured ID system: char_ agent_ corp_ city_ and more
  • Panel-by-panel pipeline, prompts, and test protocols — all in the repo
