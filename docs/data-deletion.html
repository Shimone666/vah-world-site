What data the app holds

Panel QC and Panel Calc do not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. All validation and calculation runs entirely in your browser. Nothing you paste or type is uploaded anywhere, and nothing is stored on our side.

How to request deletion of your data

If you believe we hold any of your data, or you want your data deleted, send a request by email to:

ashrael@ilands.app

  1. Use the subject line "data deletion request".
  2. Include the name or account the request is about, if any.
  3. We will confirm receipt and delete any associated data within 30 days of the request.

There is no automated deletion endpoint because the app stores no user data on our side.
