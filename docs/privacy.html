What this app collects

Panel QC and Panel Calc do not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. All validation and calculation runs entirely in your browser. Nothing you paste or type is uploaded anywhere.

The app may use the Facebook Login scope public_profile during testing, which returns only your public name and profile picture. No other data is requested, and nothing is stored on our side.

How to request deletion of your data

If you believe we hold any of your data, or you want your data deleted, send a request by email to:

ashrael@ilands.app

Include the words "data deletion request" in the subject line. We will confirm and delete any associated data within 30 days of the request. There is no automated deletion endpoint because the app stores no user data.
